About 3D Scan Pro

Pioneering the future of dimensional measurement

Founded in 2018 by a team of computer vision experts and manufacturing engineers, 3D Scan Pro emerged from a simple observation: traditional 3D scanning workflows were inefficient, error-prone, and couldn't scale with modern manufacturing demands. We recognized that artificial intelligence held the key to transforming this critical industrial process. What started as a research project in a small lab has evolved into a comprehensive AI-powered platform trusted by hundreds of companies across automotive, aerospace, medical device, and precision manufacturing industries. Our founding vision—to make professional-grade 3D scanning accessible, accurate, and efficient—remains at the heart of everything we do.

Today, 3D Scan Pro is committed to continuous innovation and customer success. We believe that technology should empower people, not replace them. Our mission is to develop intelligent scanning solutions that eliminate tedious manual work, dramatically improve accuracy, and enable teams to focus on high-value strategic initiatives. We're driven by core values of excellence, integrity, and customer-centricity. Every feature we build, every algorithm we refine, and every decision we make is guided by our commitment to delivering measurable value to our customers and advancing the entire industry toward a more efficient, accurate, and sustainable future.
